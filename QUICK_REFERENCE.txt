───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY CONCEPTS FOR NEW TEAM MEMBERS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

NAV = Net Asset Value (current unit price, changes daily)
Units = Position size (number of shares you own)
Market Value = Units × NAV (what your position is worth today)
Cost Basis = Units × Avg Price (what you paid in total)
Unrealized P/L = Market Value - Cost Basis (current gain/loss)
Realized P/L = Gains from past sales/redemptions

Time periods:
  1D = 1 day ago
  1W = 1 week ago
  15D = 15 days ago (default view)
  1M = 1 month ago

AMC = Asset Management Company (the fund provider)
SSF = 15-year superannuation vehicle (tax benefits)
ThaiESG = Environmental/Social/Governance certified
ThaiESGX = ESG + LTF (Long-term holding) benefits
